Privacy Policy

Pizza Calc collects no data whatsoever. The app has no user accounts, no logins, no authentication, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

All recipe data is stored locally on your device and synced through your personal iCloud account. This is your data, stored in your iCloud, and I have no access to it.

If you have any questions about privacy, please contact me at support@eimermusic.com.
